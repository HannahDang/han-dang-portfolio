About Me

Five years of search & recommendation, one deliberate detour to Berlin.

I spent five years as a product manager on Zhihu's Search & Recommendation team — China's leading Q&A platform, about 100M monthly active users. I was promoted four times to P9, the top individual-contributor grade at the company.

In 2023 I relocated to Berlin, right around the time I became a parent. I treated it as its own project, not a pause: working part-time as a digital strategy consultant, reaching German B2, and staying hands-on by prototyping GenAI-based automation.

What ties it together is the same instinct — take an ambiguous, data-heavy problem like how do we rank fairly, and turn it into a roadmap engineers and business stakeholders can rally behind. Now I'm looking for a Senior or Lead PM role on an international team building data- and AI-driven products in Europe.
